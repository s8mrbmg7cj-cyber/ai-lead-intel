01

Overview

AI Lead Intel ("we", "us", "our") provides AI-powered voice receptionist services to small businesses. Our service answers your business calls 24/7, captures leads, and delivers email summaries with transcripts and recordings.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the rights you have over your data. By using our website at aileadintel.com or our services, you agree to the practices described here.

02

Information we collect

We collect only what we need to deliver the service. There are three categories:

Account and onboarding information

  • Business details — business name, industry, website, business phone number, business hours.
  • Contact info — your name, email address, and notification phone number.
  • Configuration choices — call topics, AI personality, transfer numbers, pricing rules, emergency settings, and any setup notes you provide.

Call data

  • Caller phone numbers received through our service.
  • Call recordings, transcripts, and AI-generated summaries for calls answered by our AI.
  • Call metadata — date, time, duration, and outcome of each call.

Technical and usage data

  • Log data — IP address, browser type, device info, and pages visited.
  • Cookies — see Cookies & tracking below.
03

How we use it

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and improve the AI Lead Intel service.
  • Answer, transfer, and route calls on behalf of your business.
  • Send you call summaries, lead alerts, and service notifications via email.
  • Communicate with you about your account, billing, and support requests.
  • Detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, or technical issues.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not use call recordings or transcripts to train third-party AI models without your explicit consent.

04

Voice calls & recordings

AI Lead Intel is currently a voice-only service. When the AI answers a call on behalf of your business, the conversation is recorded and transcribed so you can review it in your dashboard.

What we record

  • Audio recordings of calls handled by your AI receptionist.
  • AI-generated transcripts of those calls.
  • Caller phone number and call metadata (date, time, duration).

How callers are notified

Where required by law, the AI receptionist will inform callers at the start of a call that the conversation is being handled by an AI assistant and that the call may be recorded. Recording practices comply with applicable state and federal laws.

SMS / text messaging

AI Lead Intel does not currently send marketing or promotional SMS messages. We may occasionally send transactional service-related messages (such as account updates or setup confirmations) to phone numbers you provide. We do not sell, rent, share, or transfer phone numbers to third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes. Standard message and data rates may apply for any service-related messages we do send. If you ever receive a message from us and want to opt out, reply STOP. Reply HELP for support.

05

Sharing & disclosure

We share information only when needed to run the service or when legally required. Specifically:

  • Service providers — we work with vendors like Twilio (telephony), Vapi (voice AI), Supabase (database), Vercel (hosting), and Resend (email delivery). These providers process data on our behalf under their own privacy and security commitments.
  • Legal compliance — we may disclose information if required by law, subpoena, or court order, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of AI Lead Intel, our users, or others.
  • Business transfers — if we are involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your data may transfer as part of that transaction.

We never share your phone number or contact information with third-party marketing or advertising platforms, data brokers, lead aggregators, or affiliates for any purpose. We do not sell your personal information.

06

Cookies & tracking

We use a small number of cookies and similar technologies to keep the site working, remember your preferences, and understand how the site is used.

  • Essential cookies — required for login, sessions, and basic site function.
  • Analytics cookies — help us measure traffic and improve the service. We use aggregated, non-identifying data wherever possible.

Most browsers let you block or delete cookies through your settings. If you block essential cookies, parts of the site may not work.

07

Data security

We use industry-standard practices to protect your data:

  • HTTPS/TLS encryption for all web traffic.
  • Encryption at rest for databases and call recordings.
  • Access controls limiting who on our team can view sensitive data.
  • Regular review of our service providers' security practices.

No system is perfectly secure. If we ever experience a breach affecting your data, we'll notify you as required by applicable law.

08

Data retention

We keep your data only as long as we need it to provide the service or comply with our legal obligations.

  • Account data — kept while your account is active. Deleted or anonymized within 90 days of account closure.
  • Call recordings & transcripts — kept for up to 12 months by default, unless you request earlier deletion or a different retention period.
  • Billing & tax records — kept as required by law (typically 7 years).

You can request deletion of your data at any time — see Your rights.

09

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights including:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction — ask us to fix inaccurate data.
  • Deletion — ask us to delete your personal data.
  • Restriction or objection — limit how we process your data.
  • Portability — receive your data in a portable format.
  • Withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent.

To exercise any of these rights, email hello@aileadintel.com. We'll respond within 30 days.

10

Children's privacy

AI Lead Intel is built for businesses and is not intended for children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us at hello@aileadintel.com and we'll delete it.
