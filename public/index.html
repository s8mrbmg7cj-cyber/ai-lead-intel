The problem

Why local businesses lose leads.

Most missed calls aren't lost because customers don't want to buy. They're lost because nobody responded fast enough.

Missed calls
You're on the job, not at a desk. The phone rings. You don't see it until hours later — and the lead's gone.
Slow response times
When a lead waits more than 5 minutes for a callback, they almost always move on to the next business.
After-hours inquiries
Half the calls come in nights and weekends. By Monday morning, those callers already booked someone else.
Competitors answer faster
The first business to respond usually wins the job. If you're #2 to reach out, you've already lost.
