Set up conditional call forwarding

Conditional forwarding only sends calls to your AI when you don't answer within a few rings — or when you're on another call. You still pick up live whenever you can.

  2. Dial *71 followed by your AI forwarding number (no spaces). Example: *71
  3. Press call. You'll hear a confirmation tone.
  4. Done. Verizon will now forward calls you don't answer.

How missed call forwarding works

You pick up first. When a customer calls, your phone rings normally. You can answer if you're available.

Missed it? Your AI picks up. If you don't answer within ~25 seconds, or you're on another call, the call routes to your AI. The caller never hears voicemail — they have a real conversation instead.

You get a summary. The moment the call ends, you get a clean email with their name, what they need, and how to follow up.
